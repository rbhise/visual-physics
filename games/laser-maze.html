Game for Reflection of Light

Laser Maze

Turn the mirrors to bend the laser beam around the blocks and onto the target. Each mirror sits at 45°, so every reflection turns the beam through a right angle.

How to play

  • Tap a mirror to turn it between / and \. The beam updates at once.
  • Keyboard: click the grid, use the arrow keys to choose a mirror and press Space to turn it.
  • Solve each level in as few moves as you can, then press Next level.

The physics behind it

At every mirror the beam obeys the laws of reflection: the angle of incidence equals the angle of reflection, both measured from the normal (the dashed line). Because each mirror is at 45° to the beam, \(i = r = 45^\circ\) and the beam turns through \(45^\circ + 45^\circ = 90^\circ\). A periscope uses exactly this trick with two mirrors.
