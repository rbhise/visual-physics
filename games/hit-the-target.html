Game for Laws of Motion

Hit the Target

Set the angle and the speed, press Fire, and land the ball on the green target. You get three shots for each target, and the fewer you need, the more you score.

How to play

  • Move the angle and speed sliders, then press Fire! (or press Enter on a slider).
  • A hit on the first shot scores 100, the second 60, the third 30, plus a level bonus. Miss all three and the game is over.
  • From level 4 a wall stands in the way: you will need a steeper shot.
  • Stuck? Hint works out the range for your settings, at a cost of 20 points.

The physics behind it

On level ground a ball launched at speed \(u\) and angle \(\theta\) lands a distance

$$R = \frac{u^2 \sin 2\theta}{g}$$

away, with \(g = 9.8\) m/s². The range is greatest at 45°, and two angles that add up to 90° (say 30° and 60°) give the same range. The steeper one goes higher, which is how you get over the walls.
