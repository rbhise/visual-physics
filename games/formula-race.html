Game for Measurement of Matter

Formula Race

Two ions, four formulae, one right answer. How many compounds can you get right in 60 seconds?

How to play

  • Press Start the race. Click the right formula, or press 1–4 on the keyboard.
  • Each correct answer scores 10, plus a bonus for every answer in a row. A wrong answer shows the right one and breaks your streak.
  • At the end you get a list of the compounds to revise. Practice mode has no timer.

The chemistry behind it

Write the positive ion first. Cross the valencies over: each one becomes the other ion's subscript. Simplify if both numbers share a factor, and put brackets around a radical such as OH or SO₄ when it needs a subscript. For example, Ca²⁺ and OH⁻ give Ca(OH)₂; Al³⁺ and O²⁻ give Al₂O₃.

Learn it first: build formulae step by step with the criss-cross simulation.
