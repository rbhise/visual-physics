Surface Area and Volume, lesson 3

Spheres and Hemispheres

A football, a marble, a bowl: round solids have no edges to measure, yet their surface and volume depend on just one length, the radius.

Sphere

A sphere is the set of all points in space at the same distance from a fixed point, its centre. That distance is the radius \(r\); the diameter is \(2r\). A ball is a sphere.

Surface area of a sphere

Wind a string tightly round half of a ball until it is covered, then unwind it and cover the whole ball. Now use the same length of string to fill circles with the same radius as the ball. The string that covers the ball fills exactly four circles. So the surface of a sphere is four times the area of a circle of radius \(r\).

Sphere. $$\text{Surface area} = 4\pi r^2$$ $$\text{Volume} = \tfrac{4}{3}\pi r^3$$

The sphere fits exactly inside a cylinder of radius \(r\) and height \(2r\). Its surface \(4\pi r^2\) equals the cylinder's curved surface \(2\pi r \times 2r\), and its volume is \(\tfrac23\) of the cylinder's volume \(2\pi r^3\).

Hemisphere

A plane through the centre cuts a sphere into two equal halves. Each half is a hemisphere. A solid hemisphere has a curved surface (half the sphere) and a flat circular face.

Hemisphere. $$\text{Curved surface area} = \tfrac12 \times 4\pi r^2 = 2\pi r^2$$ $$\text{Total surface area of a solid hemisphere} = 2\pi r^2 + \pi r^2 = 3\pi r^2$$ $$\text{Volume} = \tfrac12 \times \tfrac43 \pi r^3 = \tfrac{2}{3}\pi r^3$$

You can check the volume with sand. Take a hollow hemisphere and a hollow cone with the same radius and with height equal to the radius. The cone holds \(\tfrac13 \pi r^2 \times r = \tfrac13 \pi r^3\). It takes two conefuls to fill the hemisphere: \(2 \times \tfrac13 \pi r^3 = \tfrac23 \pi r^3\).

A bowl is a hollow hemisphere: it has no flat face, so only the curved surface \(2\pi r^2\) counts.

Try it

Choose a solid and a view, then move the radius slider.

  • Four circles. Sphere of radius 7 cm, π = 22/7.
  • Sphere in a cylinder. Radius 7 cm, π = 22/7.
  • Two conefuls. Hemisphere of radius 6 cm, π = 3.14.
  • Solid hemisphere. Radius 7 cm, π = 22/7.

What you should see: each circle of radius 7 cm has area 154 cm², and four of them make the sphere's surface, 616 cm². The cylinder round that sphere holds 2156 cm³; the sphere holds two thirds of it, about 1437.33 cm³. The cone with r = h = 6 cm holds 226.08 cm³ and two of them fill the hemisphere, 452.16 cm³. The solid hemisphere of radius 7 cm has curved surface 308 cm², flat face 154 cm² and total surface 462 cm².

Worked examples

1. A football

A ball has diameter 21 cm. Find its surface area and its volume. \((\pi = \tfrac{22}{7})\)

  1. \(r = 21 \div 2 = 10.5\) cm
  2. Surface area \(= 4\pi r^2 = 4 \times \tfrac{22}{7} \times 10.5 \times 10.5 = 88 \times 1.5 \times 10.5 =\) 1386 cm²
  3. Volume \(= \tfrac43 \pi r^3 = \tfrac43 \times \tfrac{22}{7} \times 10.5^3 = \tfrac{4 \times 22 \times 1.5 \times 10.5 \times 10.5}{3} = 44 \times 110.25 =\) 4851 cm³

2. A solid hemisphere

A solid hemisphere has radius 6 cm. Find its curved surface area, total surface area and volume. \((\pi = 3.14)\)

  1. Curved surface area \(= 2\pi r^2 = 2 \times 3.14 \times 36 =\) 226.08 cm²
  2. Total surface area \(= 3\pi r^2 = 3 \times 3.14 \times 36 =\) 339.12 cm²
  3. Volume \(= \tfrac23 \pi r^3 = \tfrac23 \times 3.14 \times 216 = 3.14 \times 144 =\) 452.16 cm³

3. Radius from the surface area

The surface area of a sphere is 1256 cm². Find its radius and diameter. \((\pi = 3.14)\)

  1. \(4 \times 3.14 \times r^2 = 1256\), so \(12.56\,r^2 = 1256\)
  2. \(r^2 = 1256 \div 12.56 = 100\), so \(r =\) 10 cm and the diameter is 20 cm

Common mistakes

  • Using the diameter as r. A 21 cm ball has r = 10.5 cm. Halve the diameter first.
  • 4πr³ or 4πr² mixed up. Surface area has r² (square units, cm²); volume has r³ (cubic units, cm³).
  • 2πr² for a solid hemisphere. A solid hemisphere also has a flat circle: total surface area is 3πr². A hollow bowl has only 2πr².
  • Halving the wrong formula. Hemisphere volume is half of ⁴⁄₃πr³, which is ⅔πr³, not ⅓πr³.

Quick check

1. A sphere has radius 7 cm. Its surface area (π = 22/7) is…

4πr² = 4 × 22/7 × 49 = 616 cm².

2. The total surface area of a solid hemisphere of radius r is…

Curved surface 2πr² plus the flat circle πr².

3. The curved surface area of a hemisphere of radius 10 cm (π = 3.14) is…

2πr² = 2 × 3.14 × 100 = 628 cm².

4. If the radius of a sphere is doubled, its volume becomes…

Volume has r³, and 2³ = 8.
