Surface Area and Volume, lesson 1

Cuboids, Cubes and Cylinders

A shoebox, a dice and a tin of paint: how much card covers each one, and how much space is inside? Two numbers answer that: surface area and volume.

Surface area and volume

A solid takes up space. The total area of all its faces is its surface area, measured in square units such as cm². The space it fills is its volume, measured in cubic units such as cm³. A cube of side 1 cm has volume 1 cm³.

Cuboid

A cuboid has 6 rectangular faces in 3 equal pairs. Call its length \(l\), breadth \(b\) and height \(h\).

Cuboid. $$\text{Area of the four vertical faces (lateral surface area)} = 2(l + b) \times h$$ $$\text{Total surface area} = 2(lb + bh + lh)$$ $$\text{Volume} = l \times b \times h$$

Open the Net view: the six faces lie flat, and you can see the three pairs. Turn on Show 1 cm cubes to count the cubes that fill the box: one layer of \(l \times b\) cubes, stacked \(h\) layers high.

Cube

A cube is a cuboid whose length, breadth and height are all equal. If its side is \(l\), each face is a square of area \(l^2\).

Cube. Lateral surface area \(= 4l^2\), total surface area \(= 6l^2\), volume \(= l^3\).

Right circular cylinder

A tin or a pipe is a right circular cylinder: two congruent circular bases joined by a curved surface, with the axis perpendicular to the bases. Let the radius of the base be \(r\) and the height \(h\).

Cut the curved surface straight down and unroll it. It becomes a rectangle. Its length is the circumference of the base, \(2\pi r\), and its breadth is \(h\).

Cylinder. $$\text{Curved surface area} = 2\pi rh$$ $$\text{Total surface area} = 2\pi rh + 2\pi r^2 = 2\pi r(r + h)$$ $$\text{Volume} = \text{area of base} \times \text{height} = \pi r^2 h$$

Like the textbook, each problem says which value of \(\pi\) to use: \(\dfrac{22}{7}\) (handy when \(r\) is a multiple of 7) or \(3.14\). The simulation lets you choose.

Try it

Choose a solid and a view, then move the sliders.

  • A box. Cuboid 10 cm × 6 cm × 4 cm, with its 1 cm cubes.
  • Fold it flat. The net of a cube of side 5 cm.
  • Unroll a tin. Cylinder with r = 7 cm, h = 10 cm, π = 22/7.
  • Use π = 3.14. Cylinder with r = 5 cm, h = 12 cm.

What you should see: the 10 × 6 × 4 cuboid has lateral surface area 128 cm², total surface area 248 cm² and volume 240 cm³ (240 small cubes). The cube's net is 6 squares of 25 cm², 150 cm² in all. The tin unrolls into a rectangle 44 cm by 10 cm, so its curved surface area is 440 cm². With π = 3.14, the second cylinder has curved surface area 376.8 cm², total surface area 533.8 cm² and volume 942 cm³.

Worked examples

1. A gift box

A cuboid box is 12 cm long, 8 cm broad and 5 cm high. Find the area of its four vertical faces, its total surface area and its volume.

  1. Vertical faces \(= 2(l + b)h = 2(12 + 8) \times 5 = 2 \times 20 \times 5 =\) 200 cm²
  2. Total surface area \(= 2(lb + bh + lh) = 2(96 + 40 + 60) = 2 \times 196 =\) 392 cm²
  3. Volume \(= 12 \times 8 \times 5 =\) 480 cm³

2. From surface area back to the side

The total surface area of a cube is 294 cm². Find its side and its volume.

  1. \(6l^2 = 294\), so \(l^2 = 294 \div 6 = 49\) and \(l =\) 7 cm
  2. Volume \(= l^3 = 7^3 =\) 343 cm³

3. A water tank

A cylindrical tank has radius 7 m and height 10 m. Find its curved surface area, total surface area and volume. \((\pi = \tfrac{22}{7})\)

  1. Curved surface area \(= 2\pi rh = 2 \times \tfrac{22}{7} \times 7 \times 10 =\) 440 m²
  2. Total surface area \(= 2\pi r(r + h) = 2 \times \tfrac{22}{7} \times 7 \times 17 = 44 \times 17 =\) 748 m²
  3. Volume \(= \pi r^2 h = \tfrac{22}{7} \times 49 \times 10 = 22 \times 7 \times 10 =\) 1540 m³

The simulation works in cm, but the numbers are the same: just read m for cm.

Common mistakes

  • Square units for volume. Area is in cm² (or m²); volume is in cm³ (or m³).
  • Total instead of lateral. The four vertical faces leave out the top and bottom: 2(l + b)h, not 2(lb + bh + lh).
  • Diameter for radius. If a tin is 14 cm across, r = 7 cm. Halve the diameter before using 2πrh or πr²h.
  • Forgetting the ends. Curved surface 2πrh is only the side. A closed cylinder adds two circles: 2πr(r + h).

Quick check

1. The side of a cube is 4 cm. Its total surface area is…

6l² = 6 × 16 = 96 cm². (64 is the volume in cm³.)

2. A cuboid is 5 cm × 4 cm × 3 cm. Its volume is…

l × b × h = 5 × 4 × 3 = 60 cm³. (94 cm² is its total surface area.)

3. A cylinder has radius 7 cm and height 5 cm. Its curved surface area (π = 22/7) is…

2πrh = 2 × 22/7 × 7 × 5 = 220 cm².

4. When the curved surface of a cylinder is cut and unrolled, it makes a…

A rectangle 2πr long and h wide, so its area is 2πrh.
