Linear Equations in Two Variables, lesson 3

Word Problems with Two Unknowns

Many puzzles hide two unknown numbers and two clues. Each clue becomes an equation, and solving the pair answers the puzzle.

Download slides (.pptx) 12 slides with teacher notes

From words to equations

  • Name the unknowns. Write exactly what \(x\) and \(y\) stand for, with units: “let \(x\) be the number of ₹5 notes”.
  • Turn each clue into an equation. Look for words such as total, more than, times, years ago.
  • Solve by elimination or substitution.
  • Answer the question asked, in words, and check it against the story.

Worked examples

1. Bank notes

Meena has 45 notes of ₹5 and ₹10 worth ₹350 in all. How many of each?

  1. Let \(x\) = number of ₹5 notes, \(y\) = number of ₹10 notes.
  2. \(x + y = 45\) and \(5x + 10y = 350\). Divide the second by 5: \(x + 2y = 70\).
  3. Subtract the first: \(y = 25\), so \(x = 20\).
  4. 20 notes of ₹5 and 25 notes of ₹10 (check: 100 + 250 = 350 ✓)

2. Ages

A mother and daughter are 50 years old altogether. Five years ago the mother was 7 times as old as the daughter. Find their ages.

  1. Let the mother be \(x\) years and the daughter \(y\) years now. \(x + y = 50\).
  2. Five years ago: \(x - 5 = 7(y - 5)\), so \(x - 7y = -30\).
  3. Subtract: \(8y = 80\), \(y = 10\), \(x = 40\).
  4. Mother 40 years, daughter 10 years

3. A fraction

Adding 1 to the numerator and denominator of a fraction gives 4/5. Subtracting 5 from both gives 1/2. Find the fraction.

  1. Let the fraction be \(x/y\). Then \(5(x + 1) = 4(y + 1)\), so \(5x - 4y = -1\).
  2. And \(2(x - 5) = y - 5\), so \(2x - y = 5\), i.e. \(y = 2x - 5\).
  3. Substitute: \(5x - 4(2x - 5) = -1\), so \(-3x = -21\), \(x = 7\), \(y = 9\).
  4. The fraction is 7/9

Common mistakes

  • Not saying what x and y mean. Write “let x be …” first; it stops you mixing up the unknowns.
  • Writing a two-digit number as xy. With tens digit x and units digit y, the number is 10x + y.
  • Forgetting “years ago” applies to both people. Five years ago both ages were 5 less: x − 5 and y − 5.
  • Answering with just numbers. Answer the question in words, with units, and check it in the story.

Quick check

1. The sum of two numbers is 20 and their difference is 4. The numbers are…

x + y = 20 and x − y = 4 give x = 12, y = 8.

2. A two-digit number has tens digit x and units digit y. The number is…

x tens and y units: 10x + y.

3. Pens cost ₹x and notebooks ₹y. 2 pens and 3 notebooks cost ₹80. The equation is…

2 pens cost 2x, 3 notebooks cost 3y.

4. 50 heads and 140 legs: some hens, some goats. How many goats?

h + g = 50, 2h + 4g = 140, so 2g = 40 and g = 20.
